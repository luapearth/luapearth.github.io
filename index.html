About

Ten years of shipping, not slides

I'm a software engineer with more than 10 years building software for the web and for phones. I work across the stack, but the backend is where I spend most of my time — APIs, authentication servers, and the services that hold a product together.

At Ranyan I own the backend of a janitorial management system that ships as both a web app and a mobile app: the full API, the authentication server, and the MCP the app talks to, with facial recognition used to verify users. I've also built websites for financial businesses, and pipelines that collect sensor data and turn it into dashboards.

I work on projects from small through to enterprise scale. I run the AWS infrastructure the products sit on, and I've taken apps through App Store and Play Store review.
