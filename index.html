About

Ten years of shipping, not slides

I'm a software engineer with more than 10 years building software for the web and for phones. Most of that time has been spent close to the whole stack: designing the interface, writing the services, standing up the AWS infrastructure underneath, and then getting the release through App Store and Play Store review.

I care about software that survives contact with real users — fast pages, clear error states, and deploys that don't need a war room. I've published and maintained apps on both major stores and run the cloud environments they depend on.

Right now I'm building my portfolio here, and open to product builds, mobile releases, and cloud or deployment work.

  • AWS setup and migration
  • CI/CD and release automation
  • Rescuing half-finished products
